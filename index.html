Addon Maker

Make your own skins, skin packs and add-ons on your phone or tablet, then export them as .mcpack and .mcaddon files.

Get help

Open a support request at github.com/huypoker/addon-maker/issues. Tell us your device, the app version (Settings in the app) and what happened. We answer within 3 business days.

Support requests on GitHub are public. Don't include personal information in them.

Common questions

Where are my creations? They are saved on your device. Deleting the app deletes them, so export anything you want to keep.

How do I open an exported file in the game? Tap Share after exporting and choose the game, or open the file from the Files app.

How do I cancel Maker Pass? On iPhone or iPad: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Addon Maker. On Android: Google Play › Payments and subscriptions › Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends.

I bought Maker Pass on another device. Tap Restore purchase on the Pass tab, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account.

How do I delete what I shared to Community? Settings › Delete community account. It removes your posts and nickname from our servers.

How do I change ad privacy choices? Settings › Privacy options, where your region requires it.
