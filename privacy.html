Privacy Policy

Effective 27 October 2026. Applies to the Addon Maker app for iOS, iPadOS and Android, made by Huy Ngo Nguyen Van ("we").

The short version

You don't need an account. What you make stays on your device unless you choose to share it. We use Google AdMob to show ads, Google Firebase to run our server features, and Anthropic to generate ideas you ask for. We don't sell your personal information.

What stays on your device

Your skins, packs, add-ons and settings are stored only on your device. If you save a skin to Photos, the app asks for permission to add images to your library and never reads your photos.

What reaches our servers

Our server features run on Google Firebase (Google Cloud) and are used only when available in your version of the app.

  • Anonymous ID. The app signs in with a random anonymous ID, not linked to your name, email or phone. It lets the server keep your Maker Pass status, your free idea count and your Community posts.
  • App integrity check. Firebase App Check uses Apple App Attest or Google Play Integrity to confirm requests come from the real app. It doesn't identify you.
  • Ideas. When you ask for ideas, the text you type is sent to our server and to Anthropic, which generates the ideas. Don't type personal information there.
  • Community. Only what you choose to share is uploaded: the creation, its title, and a random nickname. Likes, reports and blocks you make are stored with your anonymous ID so the feature works and so we can remove content that breaks the rules.
  • Purchases. Apple or Google handles payment. We receive the store's proof of purchase to check your subscription and store whether Maker Pass is active for your anonymous ID. We never see your payment details.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location and how you interact with ads, to show, measure and personalise ads and to prevent fraud. Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

  • On iPhone and iPad, the advertising identifier is used only if you allow tracking when the app asks. You can change this in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Where the law requires it (for example the EEA, UK and some US states), Google's consent message asks for your choices first. Change them any time in the app under Settings › Privacy options.
  • Maker Pass removes ads.

Read how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Service providers

  • Google LLC (AdMob, Firebase, Google Cloud): privacy policy
  • Anthropic PBC (idea generation): privacy policy
  • Apple and Google, for payments through their stores

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Server data stays until you delete it. Settings › Delete community account removes your posts, nickname and anonymous account from our servers. Reports about other people's posts may be kept so we can enforce the rules. Uninstalling the app deletes everything stored on your device.

Your choices and rights

You can use the app without sharing anything, turn off tracking, change ad consent, and delete your server data from Settings. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information. Contact us below for any request.

Children

Addon Maker is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has shared personal information through Community, contact us and we will delete it.
