Privacy Policy

Effective 26 November 2026. Applies to the Addon Maker app for iOS, iPadOS and Android, made by Huy Ngo Nguyen Van ("we").

The short version

You don't need an account. What you make stays on your device unless you choose to share it. We use Google AdMob to show ads, Google Firebase to run our server features, and Anthropic to generate ideas you ask for. We don't sell your personal information.

What stays on your device

Your skins, packs, add-ons and settings are stored only on your device. If you save a skin to Photos, the app asks for permission to add images to your library and never reads your photos.

What reaches our servers

Our server features run on Google Firebase (Google Cloud) and are used only when available in your version of the app.

  • Anonymous ID. The app signs in with a random anonymous ID, not linked to your name, email or phone. It lets the server keep your Maker Pass status, your AI idea credits and your Community posts.
  • Ideas. When you ask for ideas, the text you type is sent to our server and to Anthropic, which generates the ideas. Don't type personal information there.
  • Community and Explore. Only what you choose to share is uploaded: the creation, its title, and a random nickname. Posts are checked before they appear. Likes, reports and blocks you make are stored with your anonymous ID so the feature works and so we can remove content that breaks the rules. Explore also shows creations made by our team and, only with their creators' permission, add-ons from other creators.
  • AI credits. The server counts the AI ideas you use: free ideas each day, credits from Maker Pass, and credits you earn by watching an optional ad. Each change is kept for 90 days so a failed request can be refunded.
  • Purchases. Apple or Google handles payment. We receive the store's proof of purchase to check your subscription and store whether Maker Pass is active for your anonymous ID, on up to three devices. Apple and Google tell our server about renewals and refunds. We never see your payment details.
  • Ad reports. If you report an ad from Settings, we store the ad's format, Google's ID for that ad and your reason, so we can ask Google to remove it.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location and how you interact with ads, to show and measure ads, limit how often one repeats and prevent fraud. The app asks Google for non-personalised ads only. Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

  • On iPhone and iPad, the advertising identifier is used only if you allow tracking when the app asks. You can change this in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Where the law requires it (for example the EEA, UK and some US states), Google's consent message asks for your choices first. Change them any time in the app under Settings › Privacy options.
  • Optional rewarded ads. When you choose to watch an ad for AI credits, the app gives Google your anonymous ID so Google can tell our server the ad was watched and we can add the credits.
  • Maker Pass removes ads.

Read how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Service providers

  • Google LLC (AdMob, Firebase, Google Cloud): privacy policy
  • Anthropic PBC (idea generation): privacy policy
  • Apple and Google, for payments through their stores

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Server data stays until you delete it. Settings › Delete account removes your anonymous account and what our servers keep for it: posts, likes, reports you made, nickname and AI credits, and it unlinks your Maker Pass from the device (cancel the subscription in your Apple or Google account). Reports still waiting for review are kept without your ID so we can enforce the rules. Daily usage counters that limit abuse are stored under a one-way hash of your ID and are not linked back to you. Uninstalling the app deletes everything stored on your device.

Your choices and rights

You can use the app without sharing anything, turn off tracking, change ad consent, and delete your server data from Settings. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information. Contact us below for any request.

Children

Addon Maker is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has shared personal information through Community, contact us and we will delete it.
